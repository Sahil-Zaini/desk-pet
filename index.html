What it does

    

      
🚶 WandersStrolls along the bottom of the page and turns around at the edges.

      
💖 Loves petsClick it for hearts and a happy jump.

      
✋ Can be carriedDrag it anywhere and let go. It drops back down to the floor.

      
👀 CuriousMove your cursor near the bottom and it walks over to see.

      
😴 NapsAfter 30 seconds with no activity it curls up and sleeps (zzz). It wakes up when you come close.

      
💬 TalksSays little things now and then, or your own lines.

      
⤴ JumpsDouble-click it to make it jump.

      
♿ PoliteIf the visitor prefers reduced motion, it just sits and naps. It never blocks clicks on your page.
