It lives in your page

    
Most website pets just walk along the bottom. Desk Pet treats your page like a level in a game: every heading, paragraph, image and button is something to stand on.

    

      
🧗 Climbs your contentStands on the letters of your headings and jumps from paragraph to paragraph with real physics.

      
📖 Reads with youScroll the page and it hops along to the heading you're reading.

      
🖍️ Sits on selectionsSelect a sentence and the cat walks over and sits right on it, like cats do.

      
🔴 Laser pointerHold Shift and move the mouse. Cats go crazy for it and dogs join in. The camel couldn't care less.

      
🎯 Thrown with physicsDrag it and fling it. It flies, bounces off the edges and lands on whatever is below.

      
🍖 Hunger & moodAlt+click drops food: a fish, a bone or dates. It runs to eat, and remembers how you treat it.

      
👀 Watches you typeStart typing in a form and it hops onto the field to keep you company.

      
🌙 Knows the dateWears a Ramadan lantern, Eid party hat, UAE National Day flag, Santa hat or Halloween hat automatically, and gets sleepy late at night.

      
👋 SocialTwo pets on one page meet, say hi and nap together.

      
🧭 Tour guideSite owners can send the pet to a button, like "Click here for a quote!". Then it points the way.

      
🎉 Reacts to eventsIt celebrates when a form is sent, cheers up lost visitors on a 404 page and says "wait, don't go!" when someone leaves.

      
♿ PoliteShadow DOM keeps it isolated, it never blocks your page, and it stays calm for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

    

  


  

    
Three personalities

    

      
🐈 CatThe climber. It leaps high, always wants the highest heading, sits on your selected text and is obsessed with the laser.

      
🐕 DogThe runner. It's fast, follows you everywhere, gets random zoomies and is happy to be thrown again and again.

      
🐪 CamelThe calm one. It walks slowly, rarely jumps, takes long desert naps and greets you with "Marhaba!".

    

  


  

    
Desk Pet for your desktop 🖥️

    
The same pet, now on your computer. It walks along your taskbar, jumps onto your open windows and looks after you while you work.

    

      
🪟 Window hoppingClimbs on top of your open windows and rides along when you move them (Windows).

      
⏸️ Break timeEvery 50 minutes (you choose) the pet sits in the middle of a calm screen with a countdown.

      
🔒 Strict modeFor when you really need it: no skip button until the break is over.

      
💧 Water & 👀 eyesReminds you to drink water and to rest your eyes (20-20-20).

      
🍅 Focus sessionsA Pomodoro timer: the pet naps in the corner while you focus, then calls you for a break.

      
😴 Knows you're awayFalls asleep when you leave the computer and greets you when you come back.
